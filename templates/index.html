METHODOLOGY

How to read the dashboard

Performance: returns compare closing prices across defined periods. Trend: moving averages provide descriptive context. Risk: volatility measures variability while drawdown captures peak-to-trough decline. Participation: volume ratio compares the latest session with its 20-observation baseline. Risk-adjusted metrics: Sharpe, Sortino and Calmar provide contextual efficiency measures. All outputs are historical analytics, not financial advice.
