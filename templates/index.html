METHODOLOGY

How to interpret this dashboard

Returns are calculated from daily closing prices. Annualized volatility uses the standard deviation of daily returns scaled by √252. Drawdown measures the percentage decline from the running historical peak. Moving averages and volume metrics are descriptive indicators, not trading recommendations.
