# Business Questions & Dashboard Mapping

| Business question | Primary metric / analysis | Dashboard view |
|---|---|---|
| How has the asset performed? | Daily and cumulative return | Executive KPIs / Performance |
| Is the recent trend strengthening or weakening? | Close vs 20-day moving average | Price & MA chart |
| How volatile has the market been? | Annualized and rolling volatility | Risk panel |
| What has the largest historical downside been? | Maximum drawdown | Drawdown chart |
| When was trading activity unusually high? | Volume and 20-day volume comparison | Volume panel |
| How frequently were observations positive? | Up-day / down-day counts | Executive KPIs |
| Which observations deserve investigation? | Extreme returns, drawdowns and volume ratios | Decision insights |
| Is the dataset analytically reliable? | Null, duplicate, numeric and OHLC validation | Data-quality workflow |

## Decision-support principle

The dashboard translates metrics into concise observations so a stakeholder can identify periods requiring investigation. These observations are descriptive and historical; they are not trading recommendations or guarantees of future performance.
